# F1: age of the shown mean during lamp 3's move

Lavapipe, 640×360, High, Anthony's pose, sun frozen, boxes and lamps still except lamp 3. Lamp 3 moves from (12.5, 2.6, 21.5) to (14.0, 2.6, 22.5) over 40 frames. The move starts at engine frame 168–170, after the still fill. Three runs with the flag off (this branch) and three with #265 `bounded_quarters` at 1,000,000 (`96588f01`, not merged). `GENOS_LIGHTING_SC_TRACE=1` stamps the frame of each shown-mean commit and tallies the near red box. The tally is off when that variable is unset, so a shipping frame does not write the stamp.

The red box is 7,756 pixels (albedo r>0.45, g<0.35, b<0.35, distance < 6 m). A normal capture at the same pose (`n * 0.5 + 0.5`, an axis face landing on 0 or 255 in one channel) splits them into 834 axis-aligned and 6,922 slanted. That is the same 834 / 6,922 the tally prints: the 6,922 are the yawed box (`slanted`, flag 2048). They have no 12.5 cm texels by design. On this branch `lighting_world_irradiance_in` returns zero for a slanted normal, so compose shows screen probes unless the probe weight is under 0.05, in which case it falls through to the cache. On #265 High, `sh_cells` is on and those pixels read the 25 cm SH cells instead. The fine-texel age below is only the 834. The feeder age is a second 3+3, same protocol, with a stamp on the probe SH and on the 25 cm / 50 cm patch.

## What that says about the lag

On the 834, the 1M fine means are younger than flag off, not older. A quarter hold (commit only on the fourth 16-ray quarter) would leave 1M older than a path that publishes every visit. The opposite happened. The second 3+3 says the same thing: 1M fine ages at mid/stop/after are 2.8/1.0/3.9, 2.8/1.1/4.4, and 3.8/1.0/4.0, against flag off 20.6/14.6/20.8, 12.0/13.7/20.5, and 20.0/23.2/29.2. The CPU reference therefore publishes a deposit in the step it finishes (`CommitRule::Immediate`). `HoldQuarters` remains in the checker as the comparison that measurement did not select. It is a rule about those texels.

The axis-aligned darkness sits on that population: 11–15 codes at mid and stop, with the fine commit 1–4 frames old. A quarter hold is ruled out there.

The slanted darkness is also real at mid (7.3–10.5 codes, same sign on all three pairs). It is not a quarter hold either, at that mark. Flag off is showing a probe written this frame. #265 is showing a 25 cm cell under 1 frame old. The stale 25/50 cm patches on flag off are not the value those pixels show, except for the 233, which cannot account for the mean. At the stop mark the #265 cells are 11–13 frames old, so a hold is not ruled out there. This note does not identify that age as the quarter rule, and it does not explain the stop gap beyond the age that was read.
