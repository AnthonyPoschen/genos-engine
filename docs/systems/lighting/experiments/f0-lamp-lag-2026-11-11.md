Direct matches. The lag is the stored texel, not the per-pixel direct term. After the hold the two pictures agree, which is what the 4070 run did.

Over the 40 move frames, `lightingsc`:

| | Rays peak | Rays mean | `partial` | `sets` | `extra` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flag off | 1,404,928 | 712,804 | 2 | 237,686 | 332,929 |
| 1M | 1,000,048 | 768,378 | 529,188 | 160,756 | 135,539 |

1M commits 160,756 / (529,188 + 160,756) = 0.233 of its quarter visits. That is one shown set per four quarters (`partial / sets` = 3.29). It is not a stuck fourth quarter: the sets do land.

One frame of each, from the same window:

- Flag off, frame 179: `partial 0`, `sets 9506`, `extra 9506`, rays 974,096. `extra` counts a visit with `run > 1`. The set is traced and shown in that visit.
- 1M, frame 185: `partial 14225`, `sets 0`, `extra 0`, rays 773,264. Every visit that frame was one quarter, and none of them reached the fourth, so the shown mean did not change. Frame 192 is the same shape (14,243 partial, 0 sets) at 775,968 rays.

The shader does this on purpose. `lighting_cache.comp` adds the quarter into the partial sum and returns without writing the shown mean while `q < LIGHTING_SC_QUARTERS`. The mean is written on the fourth quarter, and `lighting_lamp_noted()` then sets the blend `k` to 1, so that set replaces the old mean. With the flag on, a fine texel whose lamp ring has actually moved (`lighting_lamp_ring_moved`) takes the settle young list with `ms = 1`, not `lighting_bounded_bucket`. Flag off takes the remaining quarters in one visit, so the replacement is the lamp where it is that frame. 1M shows the previous mean for up to three frames, then a set averaged across the frames the quarters were traced. That is the softer highlight. The ray totals are not the shortage: the 1M mean in the window is higher than flag off, and the peak is the ceiling rather than the 1,404,928 room.

Not changed. Showing a partial quarter, or blending the unfinished set, would be a different picture from the one #265 stores. It is not a dropped commit or a stamp that never arms: the fourth quarter lands, and the hold frame matches.

## What F1 should change

F1 is the CPU reference (tile pick, water-fill, deposit, resolve, share). It should publish a texel mean only when four quarters have been deposited, and a lamp note should replace the mean with that finished set. While a lamp ring entry has two different ends, admission in that reference is the settle young list (one quarter, hash turns), not the near-first buckets. A reference that hides this will grade F2 against the wrong curve. F1 does not patch #265. If the 1% lamp-lag gate cannot be met under a 1M ceiling, the design's next stage is F5 (path vertices), and #265 stays the fallback.

## Not in this change

The stage table also names a first-hit distance histogram and a ray-efficiency counter. Neither exists in the current `lightingsc` words, and both need a new shader counter. They are not added here.

## Networking

- Replicated state: none. The fill curve and the knockout are a local picture measurement.
- Authority: the client that shades the cache.
- Prediction / rollback: none.
- Determinism: the pictures are not a cross-device replay. Lavapipe and the 4070 are compared as numbers, not as matching pixels.
- Headless server: a server does not run this cache.
