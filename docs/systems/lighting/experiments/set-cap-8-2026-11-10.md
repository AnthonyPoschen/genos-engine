# Set cap 8 (2026-11-10)

High stores 8 sets per surface-cache texel. A texel at that cap counts as full. While the light is quiet, nothing near it has moved, and it is not stale, it is not listed for rays. Reaching the cap does not free the slot and does not restart the sets.

The coverage minimum stays the 8-set floor, clamped to the cap (`lighting_coverage_sets`). At High's cap of 8 the minimum and the hold mean are the cap. `LIGHTING_DN_MIN_SETS` is still 8. An explicit `world_cap` of 64 still packs cap 64, and the perceptual stop below that cap is unchanged.

## The old cost inversion

Draft #257 measured caps 4, 8, and 16 at about 27k steady cache rays against 104 at cap 64, with `cap_fill` on. That measurement is the scheduler, not the cost of storing fewer sets. `cap_fill` marked the cap as settled and then put every due at-cap texel on the trickle list. The due interval shrinks with estimator inconsistency, and an 8-set mean stays inconsistent, so the interval collapses toward one frame. Dormancy does not apply: it still wants 128 estimator sets, and the stored count is clamped at the cap.

On the RTX 4070, same commit `5dcb9bce`, `cap_fill` on, `world_cap` 8 against the default 64, 1440p High hall:

| | cap 64 | cap 8 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fill | 34.5 fps, 29.6 ms lighting | 41.5 fps, 24.2 ms lighting |
| Settled, frames 320–640 | 0 rays, 63 fps, 15.7 ms | 0 rays, 63 fps, 15.7 ms |
| After 200 frames | 568 rays median | about 38k rays |
| Yaw | 97k rays, 23.3 ms lighting, atrous 6.8 ms | 121k rays, 20.4 ms lighting, atrous 3.7 ms |

Capped texels were still in the due list at frame 200. Both caps do reach 0 rays once the estimator agrees, which is why frames 320–640 match. The settled 15.7 ms with 0 cache rays is mostly light/direct (6.5–6.8 ms) plus compose (3.1 ms). That is not cache work.

## After the stop

Lavapipe, 640×360, High, Anthony's pose (eye 14.5, 1.7, 23.5, yaw −0.3554, pitch 0.08), boxes still, lamps still, sun frozen, dynamic 0, time 0.12. Cap 8 is the default. Cap 64 is `--lighting-world-cap 64`. `cap_fill` is off on both. Ray counts are `lightingsc` `rays`. Lighting milliseconds are `GPU_MS` `lighting=`. Lavapipe times are ratios only. `near` / `full` are on-screen fine texels within 6 m, and how many of those are at the set cap. 95% full means `full / near` stayed at or above 0.95.

The path reference is `img/hall-path-reference.png` (72 spp). Error is near-weighted relative luminance against that PNG, sky pixels `(28, 36, 48)` masked, weight `1 / max(distance, 0.5 m)` from the depth view, on 2×2 blocks. The same measure as `scripts/lighting/oracle_metrics.py`.

Percentages are cap 8 divided by cap 64.

Near cells at cap 8 are full from frame 27 and stay full. No near cell has stored 64 sets by frame 240: the perceptual stop ends that fill first, so the cap-64 "full" count stays 0 on a still camera. The two pictures differ by 0.8% of the cap-64 near-weighted light. The floor speckle is in both. It is the direct term.

The idle atrous line is the same at both caps (dirty 0, screen mode, the same filtered count). The lavapipe lighting-total ratio is not cache rays: both medians are a few hundred rays or fewer. On the 4070, once both caps were at 0 rays, the lighting total was the same 15.7 ms.

### Yaw

80 frames still, then 0.03 rad per frame for 40 frames (frames 81–120), then the turned pose held. The saved frame is the present after the turn.

| | cap 8 | cap 64 | cap 8 / cap 64 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Peak rays through the turn | 1,404,928 | 1,404,928 | 100% |
| Mean rays through the turn | 1,274,168 | 1,274,333 | 100% |
| Peak lighting ms through the turn | 1,557 | 1,500 | 104% |
| Mean lighting ms through the turn | 1,385 | 1,368 | 101% |
| Frames until 95% of near cells are at the cap again | 225 | not by 240 | |

The peak is the boosted texel room, full: 87,808 entries × 16 rays. Both caps fill it for the whole turn. New texels, and texels the settle path marks due, outnumber that room, and each of them still takes a whole 64-ray set. The cap is how many sets a texel stores, not how many rays one set traces. Forty frames is not long enough for that difference to show up in the ray count. Cap 8's near cells are back to 95% at frame 225, 105 frames after the turn ends.

## What the yaw peak is

The 1,404,928 rays are not a property of the scheduler choice. Coverage-first and the set cap both leave them there, because the room is already full of whole sets. Coverage-first only shortens texels under the minimum to one 16-ray quarter, and that minimum is the cap itself when the cap is 8, so there is no under-minimum tier left to shorten. The texels that fill the room are the new claims and the texels a lamp change or the settle path marks due.
