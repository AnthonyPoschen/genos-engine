# ADR 0039 remaining work

Accepted design: `docs/adr/0039-lighting-simplification-one-directional-world-cache.md` (Anthony, 5 Nov 2026). Sequencing after 8 Nov: land the agreed path end to end, then refine accuracy. A quality miss on the old cell picture is parked. A numerical invalid, a leak, a hang, or a missing frame wire is a functional blocker.

Columns: **functional** means the frame path does the thing. **accuracy** and **performance** are later gates. They are not reasons to keep a superseded estimator in front of a wired replacement.

## Product defaults

High and Medium turn on `surface_cache.sh_cells`, `surface_cache.sh_texel_detail`, `surface_cache.sh_texel_ray_cut`, and `surface_cache.sh_probe_retire`. The three product bits do nothing unless `sh_cells` is on, so that store is on with them. Low keeps screen probes (ADR open question 2): all four bits stay off. Potato, DayCycle, and Gi1Bounce have no world cache, so the bits stay off there too. Each bit remains a settings key (CLI, MCP, debug menu, `set_key`). Nothing in the shader branches on the preset name.

While `lighting_lamp_settling()` is true (the drain flag, or a lamp ring entry inside `LIGHTING_WRC_CHANGE_FRAMES`), the cell mean keeps the same blend it uses when the lamp is quiet. The picture is one estimate: a stored field, or the neighbour read when no field is stored yet. The push starts that field from the neighbour read and moves it toward the filter target. There is no hold. An empty texel still shows the neighbour read. Compose does not evaluate screen-probe irradiance for a covered pixel. A lamp change that reaches a texel since it was sampled is a miss for `lighting_compact_hold_texel_rays`, so the sparse ratio does not freeze a small dim. A stale texel, a nearby move, an object-local texel, and a read that disagrees still keep their rays. The probe passes and the fine-texel relight stay for Low, for detail off, for a miss, and for a texel the cell does not agree with. Quiet frames keep the old rule: own samples are not coverage.

Frozen picture gates stay the numbers they were: blotch 0.014, cell energy 0.9, linger 0.05, lag 0.9, turn 0.403, and the contact, corner, and thin-wall ray-cut comparison. Lavapipe is not the millisecond number.

## Proof for this stage

`cargo test -p genos-render --test texel_detail_prod` on lavapipe. The dispatch is `lighting_screen_probe_indirect`, which compose calls. One test compiles that function with `LIGHTING_SC_WRITES` false and reads slots the writing compile planted, with no lamp clear. Rust `lighting_change_head` writes head 3.w, including bit 17, and the shader copies that word. A covered owner skips the probe read. Two planted corners blend by their trilinear weights. A missing corner stays out, and a black corner with samples darkens. A back-facing corner and a corner past half a cell off the plane stay out. The other side of a thin wall reads its own cell. A stale cell stays in memory and the pixel shows the probe. A slant shows the true-normal tilt and skips the probe read. One covered corner beside an own-sample corner does not skip. An unmarked parent with sun scratch 40 stays the parent. A marked seed whose owner is then emptied, after sun word z is cleared, shows the probe. A read-only compose with a marked green seed and an empty owner shows the probe colour `(1, 2, 3)` and leaves the stored seed. With texel detail on, a mover on a lit cell shows that cell at its current world position and true normal, skips the probe read, and does not allocate or change the cell. Moving it onto another colour, tilting the normal, and yawing it onto another axis read that pose's cell. A vacated static surface still shows its cell through the static path. Lamp-off, a missing corner, a black corner, a back face, and an off-plane corner follow the same accept rules as a static read. An empty cell, a stale cell, detail off, and Low keep the probes. A miss keeps the probes. Detail off with SH cells on, no probe, and a cell the parent and resample bilinear leave out (fractional sample count) shows that cell through the hit read. The same cell marked stale with texel detail on shows the probe. `diag[0]`, `diag[1]`, and `diag[2]` are displaying-compose skip, evaluate, and probe-irradiance counts for that one dispatch. Maintenance does not add. `cells_only` is not what this fixture counts: `covers()` ignores that toggle. The dispatch does not present a swapchain image. Zero presents is not a full frame. The read-only specialization is a second device: the writing compile plants the slots, that device is dropped, and the reader uploads the plant. On a 16 GiB lavapipe cgroup that reader dispatch was killed. The resident set was sampled around 9.8 GiB and the cgroup killer then reported about 14.7 GiB anonymous, including when the shader ran two rows at a time. The same kill happened for the stripped module and for the unstripped module with DontInline on large functions. That run is not a pass of the read-only specialization. The writing specialization's dispatch is the one this machine finished. The room-blotch harness does not override the High directional product, and its 0.014 gate stays parked. The live view is still `cells_only`. Thresholds are unchanged.
