# ADR 0039 remaining work

Accepted design: `docs/adr/0039-lighting-simplification-one-directional-world-cache.md` (Anthony, 5 Nov 2026). Sequencing after 8 Nov: land the agreed path end to end, then refine accuracy. A quality miss on the old cell picture is parked. A numerical invalid, a leak, a hang, or a missing frame wire is a functional blocker.

Columns: **functional** means the frame path does the thing. **accuracy** and **performance** are later gates. They are not reasons to keep a superseded estimator in front of a wired replacement.

## Product defaults

High and Medium turn on `surface_cache.sh_cells`, `surface_cache.sh_texel_detail`, `surface_cache.sh_texel_ray_cut`, and `surface_cache.sh_probe_retire`. The three product bits do nothing unless `sh_cells` is on, so that store is on with them. Low leaves those bits off until a 1060 measurement (ADR open question 2). Screen-probe passes are gone on every preset. Low and gi-1bounce still read the world cache at one bounce. Potato and DayCycle have no world cache, so the bits stay off there too. Each bit remains a settings key (CLI, MCP, debug menu, `set_key`). Nothing in the shader branches on the preset name.

While `lighting_lamp_settling()` is true (the drain flag, or a lamp ring entry inside `LIGHTING_WRC_CHANGE_FRAMES`), a noted lamp takes the new set whole and a world cell takes that set whole. The drain flag is not a note. The picture is one estimate: a stored field, or the neighbour read when no field is stored yet. The push starts that field from the neighbour read. A quiet field then steps a sixteenth toward the filter target. A lamp that left (`lighting_lamp_keep` is 0) takes the target on that push. There is no hold. An empty texel still shows the neighbour read. Compose does not evaluate screen-probe irradiance for a covered pixel. A lamp change that reaches a texel since it was sampled is a miss for `lighting_compact_hold_texel_rays`, so the sparse ratio does not freeze a small dim. A stale texel, a nearby move, an object-local texel, and a read that disagrees still keep their rays. Screen-probe passes are gone, so a miss mixes the coarser world-cache cell by trust. Quiet frames keep the old rule: own samples are not coverage.

Frozen picture gates stay the numbers they were: blotch 0.014, cell energy 0.9, linger 0.05, lag 0.9, turn 0.403, and the contact, corner, and thin-wall ray-cut comparison. Lavapipe is not the millisecond number.

## Proof for this stage

`cargo test -p genos-render --test texel_detail_prod` on lavapipe. The dispatch is `lighting_screen_probe_indirect`, which compose calls. One test compiles that function with `LIGHTING_SC_WRITES` false and reads slots the writing compile planted, with no lamp clear. Rust `lighting_change_head` writes head 3.w, including bit 17, and the shader copies that word. A covered owner skips the probe read. Two planted corners blend by their trilinear weights. A missing corner stays out, and a black corner with samples darkens. A back-facing corner and a corner past half a cell off the plane stay out. The other side of a thin wall reads its own cell. A stale cell stays in memory and the pixel shows the coarser world-cache cell when one is lit, otherwise black. A slant shows the true-normal tilt and skips the probe read. One covered corner beside an own-sample corner does not skip. An unmarked parent with sun scratch 40 stays the parent. A marked seed whose owner is then emptied, after sun word z is cleared, shows the coarser cell when one is lit, otherwise black. A read-only compose with a marked green seed and an empty owner stays black when no coarser cell is lit and leaves the stored seed. With texel detail on, a mover on a lit cell shows that cell at its current world position and true normal, skips the probe read, and does not allocate or change the cell. Moving it onto another colour, tilting the normal, and yawing it onto another axis read that pose's cell. After the mover leaves, that pose is not a mover: pose B is 8 m away and the radius is 2 m. The mover shade does not claim a world texel. The writing find then does, because the world mode is not 2. The new slot has no own samples, it is a detail candidate, and the shown value is the world cell planted at that pose. The cell key is the 25 cm size, not the 12.5 cm texel key. The cell itself is unchanged. The read-only specialization cannot claim, so the same kind of pose with no texel shows the coarser cell when one is lit, otherwise black. A covered mover calls `lighting_sh_read` from coverage and again from the display; the diag words count compose calls, not one lookup per pixel. Lamp-off, a missing corner, a black corner, a back face, and an off-plane corner follow the same accept rules as a static read. An empty cell, a stale cell, detail off, and Low show the coarser world-cache cell when one is lit, otherwise black. A miss does the same. Detail off with SH cells on, no probe, and a cell the parent and resample bilinear leave out (fractional sample count) shows that cell through the hit read. The same cell marked stale with texel detail on shows the coarser cell when one is lit, otherwise black. `diag[0]` is every displaying compose call in that dispatch. `diag[1]` and `diag[2]` stay 0. Maintenance does not add. `cells_only` is not what this fixture counts: `covers()` ignores that toggle. The dispatch does not present a swapchain image. Zero presents is not a full frame. The read-only specialization is a second process. The planter writes the slots and exits, then the reader uploads that plant and compiles only the read-only module. The module is `PROD_READONLY` with `LIGHTING_SC_WRITES` false. That compile folds the fixture's one head (High, sh_cells, texel detail, control word 64) to literals and drops the dead arms, then marks every function longer than 1000 words `DontInline`. On lavapipe LLVM 20 the folded module's pipeline creation peaked at 888388 kB. The reader then ran its assertions in 50.25 s. The fixture, planter then reader, finished in 582.65 s with a child resident-set peak of 7824716 kB, inside the 16 GiB cgroup. Before the fold the reader alone was killed (anon-rss 14711896 kB, total-vm 21216604 kB) with `lighting_world_irradiance_in` at 4650 words. `spirv-opt -O` is not used: it inlines that function to 21216 words. The room-blotch harness is unchanged: it does not set `sh_texel_detail`, and its 0.014 gate stays parked.


`cargo test -p genos-render --test probe_retire` is removed with the passes. The plant no longer dispatches place, gather, or filter.
